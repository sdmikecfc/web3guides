MODEL KOMBAT — SIMPLE ENTRANCE RELEASE
Review date: 6 October 2026

WHAT CHANGED
The homepage leads to three trading steps or two game choices: Play Arcade and
Build a Robot. The builder is Style > Parts > Finish, with all seven parts editable.
Help is optional. Rewards, token quantities and formulas live on the Rewards page.
First-time builds show affordable Tier 1 parts. Buy & finish states the coin cost.
After building, Fight with this robot is the main action. After fights, players can
buy parts to upgrade the same robot or build another. Arcade is a separate choice.
The homepage separates Join trading competition from Play mini-games, explains
Connect & trade > Track trades & rewards > Play mini-games, and shows the
60% / 15% / 15% / 10% category split. Profit, ROI and battle categories pay the
top three; volume also shares rewards among other qualifying traders.
The play entry explains Arcade Fighting and Build & Battle before opening either
game. Arcade remains unrewarded practice; eligible automatic house wins are the
current source of battle points. Reward amounts and competition scoring are unchanged. Existing robots can now
install an owned spare: the removed part returns to inventory. An in-progress
fight or repair blocks changing that robot. Current fitted value controls recycling;
old replays and finished fight records keep their original equipment.
Help links all four Strategy guides listed at https://app.doma.xyz/help and the
official MCP connection guide. Both Discord and https://x.com/sdmikecm are visible.
The site identifies @sdmike as the independent community-game organizer, with
support at https://discord.gg/doma. Eligible Doma domain-token pairs against USDC
or ETH are described consistently; ETH/USDC itself does not count.

SIGN-IN
The invalid-nonce bug is fixed with cryptographically random SIWE-compatible
codes. Expiry, signature verification and single use are retained. This fix
requires no SQL migration. Wallet connection is separate from competition entry.
The Model Kombat chooser now retains MetaMask even without an installed extension,
alongside Rabby, Coinbase, Brave, Phantom and discovered browser wallets. Its
configuration is isolated from other games. Installed MetaMask uses its direct
browser provider; mobile app handoff uses the existing MetaMask SDK.
WalletConnect/Rainbow QR connections require a valid public
NEXT_PUBLIC_WALLETCONNECT_PROJECT_ID at build time. This local preview has none;
it offers MetaMask installation guidance instead of presenting a broken QR code.
